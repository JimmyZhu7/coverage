Terms

The short, honest version.

What Coverage is

Coverage is two things: a free, shared feed of recruiting opportunities and deadlines that we scrape and verify centrally, and a private CRM for tracking your own outreach. The feed is a convenience. We work to keep it accurate and we label how confident and how fresh each date is, but you're responsible for confirming a deadline before you rely on it.

Your account

You sign in with a social provider, or an email address and password. Keep your login to yourself; you're responsible for activity under your account. Don't upload data you don't have the right to hold, and don't use Coverage to harass anyone or to break the law.

Your data belongs to you

The contacts and touches you put into Coverage are yours. You can export them whenever you like, and you can delete your account and everything in it yourself, immediately. We don't hold your data hostage.

What we ask of you around capture

The capture address logs the mail you BCC or forward to it. Only send us mail you're entitled to: your own outreach and the replies to it. See the Privacy page for exactly what we do with it.

No warranty, and limits

Coverage is provided as-is, without warranties. Dates can be wrong; connectors can break. We do our best and we flag staleness honestly, but we're not liable for a missed deadline or an outreach decision you made using the app. Confirm anything that matters at the source.

Changes

We may update these terms as the product grows. If we change something that materially affects you, we'll say so rather than bury it.
