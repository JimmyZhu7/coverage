{% comment %} Shell styles for the allauth-served account pages linked from Settings' Sign-In & Security card: password change/set, email management, and connected accounts. Why this file exists: those routes have always worked, but nothing overrode their templates, so allauth served its own raw defaults — black text on Coverage's dark background, a bare "Menu:"
    , no nav, no shell. They were reachable only by typing the URL, and looked broken when you got there. The Settings page now links to all of them, so they had to become real pages first. Deliberately small: these pages are forms and lists, and `base.html` plus the existing `.panel` / `.btn` / `.msg` tokens already do most of the work. What's here is the handful of rules those tokens don't cover. Nothing is rebranded and no token is redefined. {% endcomment %}
