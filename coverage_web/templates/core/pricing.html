The deal, plainly.

Everything in Free stays free. Paid tiers will exist because syncing inboxes and serving campuses costs real money to run. Export or delete everything, on any plan, at any time.

Do I need an account to see opportunities?

No. The feed is public. An account adds the CRM: the Today queue, the Network board, and email capture.

Will the free plan shrink when paid tiers launch?

No. Everything listed in Free today stays in Free. Paid tiers are new capabilities, not repackaged ones.

What would a Team plan look like for my club?

Shared tiers, shared coverage, and per-seat billing managed by officers. If you run a finance society, we want your input before it ships.

What happens to my data if I stop using Coverage?

Export it as CSV whenever you like, or delete your account yourself and everything goes with it, immediately.
