Two of the four layers carry a real time: a chat captured off an invite has the DTSTART the organiser sent, and a hand-added event has whatever was typed into the Time field. The other two never can. A confirmed firm date and a tracked role's closing date are DATES, stored and shown as all-day, and inventing 9am for them would put a fake appointment on the page. So the day is drawn in two parts rather than one. Everything without a time sits in an "All day" band at the top, said plainly. Everything with a time sits on the hour rail below it, in the hour it starts. Nothing is placed by guess. The rail spans 8am to 9pm and widens to whatever the day actually holds (see `_hour_rail`), so an empty evening costs a few thin rules and a 7am flight is still on the axis rather than off the end of it.
