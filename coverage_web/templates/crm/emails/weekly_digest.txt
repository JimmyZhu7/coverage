`(reported)` rides in the countdown line, spelled out, for the same reason the
.ics feed puts it in the SUMMARY rather than the DESCRIPTION
(crm/calendar_views.py): the surface has no hover. Every OTHER surface marks a
prose-read date with a dotted underline plus a `title` carrying
`item.reported.why` (_card.html, _rolecard.html, _role_drawer.html, _scope.html,
_apps_body.html, _calendar_event.html); in an inbox both of those convey exactly
nothing, and a mail client's sanitizer would strip the styling anyway. So the
marker is words, in both halves of this pair.

`item.reported` is `directory.views.deadline_provenance` — set only when
`Opportunity.confidence` is below 1.0, i.e. Coverage read the date out of the
posting's own prose instead of quoting a field the board published. On the live
board that is 96% of dated open campus roles, so a digest that dropped the
marker was presenting our own reading as the firm's statement on nearly every
row it printed.
