WHY COMPACT DROPS THE FIRM NAME FROM ITS OWN PROSE. On a My Applications card the firm is already the first line of the card, in caps, three lines up. The full form said it twice more — in the head and again in the "N more at X" link — so one 300px card printed "Bank of America" three times. Compact says "here" instead and keeps the firm name on the `title` of each, the same moved-to-a-hover call this page's location cap and stage pill already make. The drawer and the firm page pass no `compact` and are untouched: there the block stands alone, with no card around it naming the firm. {% endcomment %}
