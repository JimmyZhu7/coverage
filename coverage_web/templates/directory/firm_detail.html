Labelling those words fixed how they READ and not what they CLAIM, and bare, they claimed the wrong thing. firms.yaml's regions and tracks are a coverage declaration — where and what this firm recruits, in general. The rows below are a fact: what is open today, each one stamped with its own market by _card.html. Measured over the corpus, 25 of the 42 firms that declare a region have a top live market they never declared, and 13 have no live row in ANY declared market. This is the 925-vs-13 defect in the section below, with markets instead of counts: two true statements answering different questions, and nothing saying which was which. The prefix says which. It is one word rather than a live-market line because the rows already carry that (and `_open_markets` prints it in aggregate the moment the cap hides any), and because the declaration must survive the 39 firms whose campus scope is empty, where it is the only thing that can be said at all. {% endcomment %}
